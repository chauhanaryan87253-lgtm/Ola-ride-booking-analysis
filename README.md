# 🚖 Ola Ride Booking Analysis

## 1. Project Title / Headline

📊 **Ola Ride Booking Analysis | SQL + Advanced Excel + Power BI Data Analytics Project**

An end-to-end operational analytics project designed to evaluate ride completion rates, analyze cancellation patterns, track revenue trends, and evaluate customer-driver feedback metrics.

## 2. Short Description / Purpose

The Ola Ride Booking Analysis project explores real-world transactional ride data to uncover key operational trends and revenue drivers. By leveraging SQL, Advanced Excel, and Power BI, this project provides actionable insights into ride success rates, driver/customer cancellation reasons, booking distributions, and performance metrics across different vehicle types and cities to support data-driven decision-making.

## 3. Tech Stack

The analysis and dynamic dashboard were built using the following tools and technologies:

* 🗄️ **SQL** – Used for data extraction, aggregations, window functions, CTEs, and complex joins to query raw ride data.
* 📊 **Advanced Excel** – Utilized Power Query for cleaning, along with Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, lookup functions, and conditional formatting for initial data exploration.
* 📈 **Power BI** – Used for data modeling, DAX measures, KPI creation, and developing interactive visual reports.
* 📐 **Data Modeling & DAX** – Formed table relationships and calculated metrics to analyze booking rates, completion ratios, and customer ratings seamlessly.
* 📁 **File Format** – `.pbix` for dashboard reporting, `.sql` for data transformation scripts, and `.xlsx` for raw analysis.

## 4. Data Source

* **Source**: Ola Ride Booking Dataset.
* **Details**: Contains transactional records including booking IDs, dates/times, pickup and drop locations, vehicle types, booking status (completed, cancelled by customer, cancelled by driver), payment methods, ride values, customer ratings, and driver feedback scores.

## 5. Features / Highlights

### Business Problem

Ride-hailing services face challenges regarding high cancellation rates, fluctuating demand across peak hours, inefficient driver allocation, and inconsistent customer ratings. Identifying the root causes behind cancelled rides and regional revenue gaps is crucial for improving user retention and operational efficiency.

### Goal of the Project

To transform raw ride transactional data into structured insights, identify underlying reasons for ride cancellations, analyze revenue distribution across payment channels and vehicle types, and present an interactive dashboard tracking key operational KPIs.

### Walk Through of Key Visuals

* **Booking Success & Cancellation Matrix**: Breakdown of total bookings, completed trips, customer cancellations, and driver cancellations alongside key cancellation reasons.
* **Revenue & Ride Trends**: Time-series charts tracking overall revenue growth, booking volumes, and seasonal/hourly demand spikes.
* **Vehicle & Payment Distribution**: Visual breakdown of ride volumes categorized by vehicle types (e.g., Sedan, Auto, Mini) and preferred payment methods.
* **Geographic & Time Performance**: Spatial and hourly analysis of ride completion rates, pickup locations, and city-wide performance metrics.
* **Customer & Driver Rating Tracker**: Scatter plots and scorecard views evaluating customer rating distributions and driver service scores.

### Business Impact & Insights

* **Cancellation Reduction**: Pinpoints top customer and driver cancellation drivers to assist in refining dispatch algorithms and penalty policies.
* **Fleet & Demand Optimization**: Highlights high-demand time slots and vehicle choices to help optimize driver allocation during peak hours.
* **Revenue Strategy**: Tracks payment channel distribution and city-level earnings to guide targeted promotions and incentive structures.
